# Announcements

The Announcements library is a standalone list of print-oriented notices — separate from any
on-screen announcement slides in the [Slide Library](/slides), since a printed announcement
usually says more than a slide has room for. Your [Bulletin](/bulletin)'s optional Page 2 pulls
its Announcements section straight from this library.

## Two kinds of entries

- **Event** — has a date (and optionally an end date/time), and shows up under "Upcoming" as it
  approaches.
- **Ongoing** — a standing notice that runs from a start date until an explicit stop date you set.

## Managing the list

Filter by status (Active or Past), year, and month, or search the announcement text directly.
Announcements aren't tied to a specific service — add one once, and it's available to every
bulletin until it expires.
