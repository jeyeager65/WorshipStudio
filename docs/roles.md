# Roles

Roles are the catalog of jobs people can be assigned to — sound tech, greeter, vocalist, and so
on — organized into named **role categories** (for example, a "Praise Team" category holding
"Guitar" and "Vocals").

![Roles screen, showing role categories in the left column and a selected category's roles on the right](/screenshots/roles.webp)

This catalog feeds several other areas: [Assignments](/assignments) groups its roster by
category, [Service Templates](/service-templates) use roles for staffing slots, and
[People](/people) use them for preferred-role matching and category badges.

Roles themselves are pure catalog management — add and organize the categories and roles your
church actually uses, and everywhere else in the app picks them up automatically.
