# Assignments

Assignments track who's serving in a given [service](/services) — from the person preaching to
the volunteers running sound, and everyone in between.

## Two kinds of roles

- **Order of Service** roles are tied directly to an item in the service — for example, the
  Preacher role attached to the sermon. These follow the order of the service itself.
- **Service Team** roles are general staffing — sound, greeters, nursery, and so on — grouped by
  role category.

Use **+ Add Role** to add an extra role that isn't already part of the service, or **Reset from
Template** to bring the Service Team roles back in sync with the [Service Template](/service-templates)
this service started from (it previews the additions and removals before applying, and never
touches Order of Service roles).

## Assigning people

Each role can have multiple people added to it. The person picker favors people whose preferred
roles match, but you can assign anyone. Mark someone **Tentative** if they haven't confirmed yet,
or add a brand-new person on the spot without leaving the page.

Worship Studio warns you about scheduling conflicts automatically: an amber warning if someone is
double-booked across two roles in the same service, and a red flag if someone has marked
themselves unavailable for that date (see [People](/people)).

## Sharing the roster

**Share Assignments** builds a draft email — subject and body filled in with everyone's roles and
names — that you can open directly in your email app or copy to paste elsewhere. Worship Studio
never sends the email itself.
