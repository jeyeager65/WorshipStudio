# Services

A **service** is one week's order of worship — everything from the opening song to the closing
announcement — built once and then run live from the same place.

## Finding and creating services

The Services landing page has three tabs: **Schedule** (today's service plus the next two
weeks), **Plan Ahead** (every upcoming service, grouped by month), and **Browse** (search and
filter your whole history by type, preacher, or Bible book). **Create Service** lets you pick a
date, time, and service type, optionally start from a [Service Template](/service-templates),
and jot down an initial sermon title, passage, or preacher.

Tap or click a service to open it. Each card's **⋯** menu holds **Open Service** and **Delete
Service** — the same menu the [Song](/songs) and [People](/people) cards use.

![Services landing page's Schedule tab, listing the services coming up in the next two weeks, each with its template, song and assignment counts, a Plan button, and a ⋯ menu](/screenshots/services-schedule.webp)

## Planning a service

The **Service Plan** view is a lighter planning surface for the week or two before a service:
sermon details and outline, a scripture placeholder to fill in later, a song list you can build
from the [Song Library](/songs), and a summary of who's assigned to what. **Apply Template** can
seed (or reset) the plan's structure from a Service Template at any point.

![Service Plan view](/screenshots/service-plan.webp)

## Running a service

The full **Service Workspace** is where you build out and then run the service. The left-hand
**Service Order** lists every item: Songs, Scripture, Slides, Media, Video, an External App
hand-off, the Sermon, and print-only Bulletin Notes. A readiness check flags anything that would
block presenting — an unresolved scripture reference, a missing media file, an unassigned role —
with a click-through to fix it.

To **reorder or remove** items, use the pencil button at the top of the Service Order. Drag
handles and a remove button appear on each item, and the check button puts the list back to
normal. Keeping them behind a toggle means an ordinary tap always just selects an item — which
matters most mid-service, when the list is what you're navigating rather than editing.

When it's time to present, **Start Presenting** sends output to your audience display, and a
transport bar steps through every slide in the service with Next/Previous, Blank Screen, and
Background Only controls — see [Presenting](/presenting) for running a service live, including
the keyboard shortcuts and how to work in the plan without disturbing what's on screen.
