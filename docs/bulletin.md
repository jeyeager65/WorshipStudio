# Bulletin

The Bulletin view turns a [service](/services)'s order of worship into a printed handout, built
directly from the items already in the service — no separate document to maintain.

## Styles and export

Choose between a **Classic** or **Modern** layout. From there you can:

- **Open PDF** — available in both styles.
- **Open Word** — a `.docx` you can fine-tune further, available for the Classic style.
- **Copy** — copies both formatted and plain-text versions to your clipboard, available for the
  Classic style.

## Page 2 (optional)

Turn on a second page to include **Upcoming** events, **Announcements** (pulled straight from
your [Announcements](/announcements) library), and a **Serving Schedule** table comparing this
week's and next week's assignments by role.

## Footer content

Page 1 and page 2 each have an editable footer — the title and whether it's shown at all are
configured in **Bulletin Settings**, and the content is saved back onto the service.

Your church name, colors, and logo (see [Settings → Branding](/settings)) appear automatically in
the exported document.
